Local browser utility

Batch repair CgBI PNGs to RGBA.

Drop iOS-optimized PNGs here. The tool removes CgBI/iDOT data, inflates raw DEFLATE image data, swaps BGRA to RGBA, reverses premultiplied alpha, and writes normal PNGs. Files never leave your device.

Supports 8-bit RGB/RGBA CgBI images, including Adam7 interlacing.
