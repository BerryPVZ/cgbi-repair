Ready

IPA inspection supports ordinary ZIP/IPA archives with stored or deflated entries, XML or Apple binary Info.plist files, thin and fat Mach-O executables, and automatic CgBI scanning. Large CgBI repairs use background workers and buffered ZIP writes. Converted textures and CAF/WAV audio work offline; MP3 may require internet to load the third-party encoder. Encrypted asset decoding requires the correct game-specific key, and does not remove Apple FairPlay protection.
